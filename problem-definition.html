From Decision Problem to Research Problem

Lecture 1 · Introduction to Market Research · slides 16 to 26
Your task:
A manager tells you what they want to do. Your job is to turn that into what you need to know. Practise the step that, according to Butler (1994), decides whether all the later research effort is wasted.
Idea to discover: a management decision problem asks what should be done. A marketing research problem asks what information is needed, and looks for causes, not symptoms.
