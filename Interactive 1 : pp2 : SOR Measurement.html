What to take away

  • Stimuli and responses are usually objective: prices, ad spots, purchases and visits can be counted.
  • Intervening variables such as attitudes, satisfaction, identification and intentions live in the customer's mind. They are constructs and must be operationalized through indicators.
  • An intention is not a behaviour. Asking about it needs a construct; recording what people actually buy does not.
  • Do not invent items: take validated scales from the literature. Careful measurement pays, as customer-company identification predicted purchases where satisfaction did not.
